## 5. Why the helper exists (no library change in this branch)

`examples/_panos_bundle_helper.py`:

- `preserve_cache(dbg)` — call INSIDE `with NeuralDbg(...)` before exit.
  Copies `dbg.disk_cache._files` to `examples/panos_bundle/tensor_cache/`.
- `patch_package(json_path, mapping, extra)` — after export. Rewrites every
  `metadata.tensor_cache_path` to the bundled relative path; on miss sets
  `tensor_cache_path: null` + `tensor_cache_status: NOT-CHECKED (...)` and
  keeps the original in `tensor_cache_requested_path` (TrainProof vocabulary).
  Merges `extra` under top-level `repro` and mirrors the file into the bundle.

A proper library fix (relative paths + opt-in persist flag instead of
unconditional `cleanup()` on exit) is out of scope for this bundle branch.
